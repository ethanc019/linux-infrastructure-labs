Incident Summary - Lab 09 Log Investigation

Issue:
Apache service activity was reviewed after a controlled stop/start event.

Evidence:
- apache2 was stopped and started during the lab.
- journalctl showed Apache service lifecycle events.
- auth.log showed sudo activity for systemctl commands.

Action Taken:
- Reviewed Apache service status.
- Checked Apache logs with journalctl.
- Checked sudo/admin activity in /var/log/auth.log.
- Verified Apache returned to active running state.

Result:
The service activity was identified and summarized using Linux logs.
